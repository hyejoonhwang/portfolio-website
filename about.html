I'm a graduate student at NYU ITP in Brooklyn, designing interactive products and experiences that feel clear in real life.

I'm drawn to invisible problems. I usually start by watching how people actually move through a space or make a decision, then dig into messy research until I can see where things break down and how to fix them.

I work best when I understand the technical side behind what I'm designing, so I stay close to implementation through rapid prototyping and building with code when needed.
